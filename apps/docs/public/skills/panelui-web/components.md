# Components

Generated from the collection's source. Read a component's own file before using it: the props in it are the real ones.

## Actions

- [Button](components/button.md) — Something to press, in the weight its action deserves, with a spinner for while it works.
- [Button Group](components/button-group.md) — Related buttons joined into one control, with separators, text cells and fields between them.
- [Toolbar](components/toolbar.md) — A strip of tools and actions for an editor, with one tool chosen at a time.

## Forms and input

- [Checkbox](components/checkbox.md) — One choice that is on or off, with a tick that draws itself and a parent box for a group.
- [Chip](components/chip.md) — A pill that can be pressed: a filter turned on and off, or an added value with a × to take it out.
- [Choice Cards](components/choice-cards.md) — One option chosen from a few, each shown as a picture of what it does.
- [Combobox](components/combobox.md) — A text field that filters a list of options as you type.
- [Field](components/field.md) — A form control with its label, a line of help and its error, wired to each other.
- [Input](components/input.md) — A single line to type into, with icons, units and buttons set inside its box.
- [Label](components/label.md) — The name of a field, with its required mark, a hint and an action on one line.
- [Radio Group](components/radio-group.md) — One choice from a short list, with every option visible and the arrow keys moving between them.
- [Select](components/select.md) — A button that opens a list of options and shows the one chosen.
- [Slider](components/slider.md) — A number set by dragging along a track, with its name and value written inside it.
- [Switch](components/switch.md) — On or off, for a setting that takes effect the moment it changes.
- [Textarea](components/textarea.md) — A field for more than one line that grows as it is written in, up to a limit.
- [XY Pad](components/xy-pad.md) — Two numbers set with one drag: a point moved across a field.

## Overlays

- [Alert Dialog](components/alert-dialog.md) — A question that must be answered before the page can be used again, with the answers side by side under it.
- [Command](components/command.md) — A launcher: type to find a page, an app or an action, and run it with Enter.
- [Command Dock](components/command-dock.md) — A small button that opens into a field, with the answer arriving under it a row at a time.
- [Dialog](components/dialog.md) — A task done in a panel over the page, with its buttons in the band beneath it.
- [Menu](components/menu.md) — A list of actions that opens from a button, with shortcuts, checks and submenus.
- [Sheet](components/sheet.md) — A panel that rises from an edge of the screen, floating clear of it, and leaves with a swipe.
- [Tooltip](components/tooltip.md) — A short hint about a control while it is hovered or focused: a pill, a card, its keys or a status.

## Navigation

- [Breadcrumb](components/breadcrumb.md) — The trail of links back up a hierarchy to the page you are on, with the middle folded away when the path runs long.
- [Nav Rail](components/nav-rail.md) — A collapsible sidebar of destinations that keeps one active pill sliding between rows, and folds to a column of icons with tooltips.
- [Pagination](components/pagination.md) — Page controls for a long result set: the pages near you, the gaps between them, and a way across.
- [Scroll Header](components/scroll-header.md) — A page title with room around it that hands over to a compact sticky bar as the reader scrolls.
- [Steps](components/steps.md) — A flow that moves one stop at a time, its numbered stops joined by a line that fills as far as you have got.
- [Table of Contents](components/table-of-contents.md) — The sections of a long page as a quiet rail, with one mark that moves to what you are reading.
- [Tabs](components/tabs.md) — A few views of one thing, one at a time, chosen from a row of tabs whose marker slides to the one chosen.

## Layout and structure

- [Canvas](components/canvas.md) — A surface of dots, lines or crosses for other components to sit on, with a light that can follow the pointer.
- [Card](components/card.md) — A surface that holds one thing, with a heading, an action beside it and a footer.
- [Frame](components/frame.md) — A panel of rows set into a tray, with the tray's top strip carrying the title.
- [Inspector](components/inspector.md) — The settings panel beside an editor's canvas: a rail of icons, each opening a page of titled sections.
- [Lane Board](components/lane-board.md) — Lanes of cards that can be dragged within a lane and from one lane to another.
- [Tilt Card](components/tilt-card.md) — A card that leans towards the pointer, with layers inside it that move by their depth.
- [Workbench](components/workbench.md) — The frame of an editor: a canvas with the work on it, a toolbar over it, an inspector beside it and a timeline under it.

## Data

- [Avatar](components/avatar.md) — A person or a team as a picture, or their initials in a colour of their own, with a badge and a group that opens up.
- [Badge](components/badge.md) — A short label for a state or a kind, tinted in its theme colour, with a dot for a state that is still changing.
- [Bar Picker](components/bar-picker.md) — A short run of values as columns of lights, one column picked.
- [Day Grid](components/day-grid.md) — A run of days as a field of dots, each sized by how much happened on it.
- [Flow](components/flow.md) — A canvas of nodes joined by edges, that you pan, zoom and rearrange.
- [KPI](components/kpi.md) — One number and what it is doing: its change, its shape over time, its progress to a target.
- [Planet](components/planet.md) — The world as dots on a globe you can turn, with markers pinned to it.
- [Range Meter](components/range-meter.md) — A reading on a ruler of ticks that rises where the value is, with the number above it.
- [Rolling Number](components/rolling-number.md) — A number whose digits roll to their new values.
- [Stat](components/stat.md) — One figure written large, with a label over it, a unit after it and a badge beside it.
- [Table](components/table.md) — Rows of records with the same columns, with numbers aligned and a header that stays pinned.

## Charts

- [Area Chart](components/area-chart.md) — Filled bands over time, stacked or overlaid.
- [Bar Chart](components/bar-chart.md) — Categories compared by length, grouped or stacked.
- [Bubble Chart](components/bubble-chart.md) — One labelled circle per row, on two measured axes, with a third quantity on each circle's area.
- [Bump Chart](components/bump-chart.md) — How a set of things ranked against each other over time.
- [Candlestick Chart](components/candlestick-chart.md) — Open, high, low and close for a period, drawn as one mark.
- [Funnel Chart](components/funnel-chart.md) — Where a population drained away, one step at a time.
- [Heatmap Chart](components/heatmap-chart.md) — A calendar of bins, shaded by how much happened in each.
- [Hex Chart](components/hex-chart.md) — A whole broken into parts, counted out in hexagonal cells.
- [Line Chart](components/line-chart.md) — A series over time, drawn in from the left.
- [Live Line Chart](components/live-line-chart.md) — A reading that keeps arriving, against a window that keeps moving.
- [Mirror Area Chart](components/mirror-area-chart.md) — Two readings of one timeline, one above a baseline and one below it.
- [Pie Chart](components/pie-chart.md) — One total, divided between its parts.
- [Plot](components/plot.md) — A chart you assemble from marks on one shared scale, for the shapes the named charts do not cover.
- [Polar Area Chart](components/polar-area-chart.md) — Several readings compared on one measure, as a dial.
- [Pyramid Chart](components/pyramid-chart.md) — Two series mirrored about a centre line, one row per category, on one shared scale.
- [Radar Chart](components/radar-chart.md) — Several measures of one thing, drawn as one shape.
- [Ring Chart](components/ring-chart.md) — Progress towards several targets, as concentric arcs.
- [Sankey Chart](components/sankey-chart.md) — Where a quantity came from and where it ended up, as ribbons between stages.
- [Scatter Chart](components/scatter-chart.md) — Two measured quantities against each other, to show how they relate.
- [Treemap Chart](components/treemap-chart.md) — A total, cut into the parts it is made of, sized by area.
- [Waterfall Chart](components/waterfall-chart.md) — How a run of changes carried one total to another, with each step floating on the running balance.

## Feedback and status

- [Alert](components/alert.md) — A message set into the page that stays until it is dealt with, as a card in a tray of its tone.
- [Feedback](components/feedback.md) — A dialog with a well to write in and two actions in the band around it.
- [Not Found](components/not-found.md) — An error code drawn in pixels that come apart near the pointer, with a title and a way out.
- [Spinner](components/spinner.md) — Something working that has not finished: an arc, spokes, dots, an orbit, a pulse or a dither cell.
- [Toast](components/toast.md) — A short message that leaves on its own, with a title that changes through dither.

## Media and motion

- [Timeline](components/timeline.md) — Time laid out left to right: tracks of clips under a ruler, with a playhead.

## AI components

- [Message](components/message.md) — One turn of a conversation with an assistant: a person's bubble, or an answer set as prose with actions under it.
- [Model Picker](components/model-picker.md) — A select for the model a message goes to, searched by name, filtered by provider and starred.
- [Prompt](components/prompt.md) — A field for writing to an assistant, with its files, model and tools in one card.
- [Soundwave](components/soundwave.md) — A voice drawn while an app listens: capsules, bars, a line, a field of dots, spokes round a person, or rings of dots.
- [Usage](components/usage.md) — How full a model's context or a plan is, as a small indicator that opens into the figures.
